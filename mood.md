# Mood

**Walk India's heritage** should feel like being let into an old stone temple by someone who
loves it: warm, quiet, crafted, a little reverent. Not a museum kiosk, and not a game.

## Keywords

Sunlit sandstone · lamplight · carved, not printed · gold leaf on dark stone · palm-leaf and
parchment · stillness · Odisha's coast (the sea wind, casuarinas, coconut palms).

## Do

- Let the stone and the light carry the screen; the interface sits lightly on top.
- Make things *by hand* in the scene: carved maps, raised models, wheels and lotus marks.
- Use the gold sparingly, for what is live or what to do next.
- Move slowly and smoothly: carving, rising, gliding. Nothing pops or snaps.
- Keep real places real: photos only in the story panels, and every fact sourced.

## Don't

- No flat corporate UI chrome, bright blues, neon or pure white.
- No photo textures on the 3D model; no clip-art or stock icons in place of carved motifs.
- No toy-like shapes (lollipop trees, primary colours).
- No busy screens: one clear thing to look at, one clear thing to do.
